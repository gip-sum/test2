# StepIndicator

Progress through the six-step posting wizard, with the autosave reassurance that keeps sellers from abandoning.

The posting flow is the supply side of the marketplace. Its completion rate is the product's most important seller metric, and the target is **under 8 minutes on a phone**.

**The six steps**: Basics · Location · Profile · Photos · Pricing · Preview.

**Rules**

- **The draft is created server-side on the first field blur**, not at the end. Losing a half-finished form is the biggest single cause of abandonment.
- **The autosave state is always visible.** "Saved just now" is reassurance that measurably reduces drop-off.
- Completed steps are tappable; future steps are not.
- On phones the indicator condenses to "Step 3 of 6" plus a progress bar — six labelled steps do not fit at 390px.
- The **completeness score** appears from step 4 and names the single highest-value missing field, because that is the cheapest available lever on listing quality.

**Consumer provides**: current step, completed steps, save state and the completeness score.
